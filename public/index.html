Reconcile from bank screenshots

Upload screenshots of one account — the balance header plus the transaction list. The app reads them, matches what it finds against that account's calendar, and shows you what to confirm, add, or check off. Nothing changes until you review and press Apply, and Apply can be undone from History. Screenshots are sent to an AI service to read the text, then discarded — they are not stored.
